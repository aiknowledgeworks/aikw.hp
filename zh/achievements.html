02 — 案例

案例 详情

培训案例A

Introduced generative AI training (Starter) for a sales department. Participant attitudes changed significantly, and 95% said they wanted to use AI in their work going forward. Proposal and email creation time was reduced by 50%, improving sales efficiency.

培训案例B

Introduced generative AI training (Starter) to improve internal meeting efficiency. Minutes summarization time was reduced by 80% compared with manual work, reducing post-meeting burden and enabling teams to focus on more essential work.

培训案例C

Delivered generative AI training (Expert) and uncovered deeper organizational needs. We proposed digitizing paper-based internal materials and turning them into a chatbot, now under consideration, with the goal of improving knowledge sharing and accessibility.

培训案例D - 全面AI导入项目

A case where training developed into full AI adoption support. We supported construction of a company-specific chatbot and internal systems using a local LLM, balancing operational efficiency with information security in a secure environment.

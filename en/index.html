02 — Company

Making what people love possible.

AI Knowledge Works was founded in Osaka in 2025 to help people use AI as a tool for self-realization, not as something reserved for experts.

We combine practical AI education, Microsoft solution training, and security awareness to help organizations move from curiosity to confident adoption.

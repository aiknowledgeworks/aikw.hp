AI Manabi Lab.

A translated English version of the Japanese volunteer page, centered on learning, creativity, and safe AI exploration.

01 — Activity

Learning through AI and creation.

AI Manabi Lab is a volunteer activity that helps children, students, and local communities experience AI as something they can touch, test, and create with.

Exploring

Trying AI tools and learning how they respond.

Creating

Turning ideas into images, text, slides, apps, and prototypes.

Collaborating

Learning together in workshops and community settings.

02 — Purpose

Why we do it.

01

Profile

In the autumn of his third year after high school, he experienced severe depression and spent a period unable to get out of bed. He gradually recovered and went on to study law at Sophia University.

His path has had many detours. That is why he now uses AI, learning, and words to help people take their next step.

People can change at any age.

He provides practical AI training that combines Microsoft tools such as PowerPoint and Teams with generative AI such as Copilot and Claude. He also draws on Microsoft 365 administration and cybersecurity knowledge to teach safe AI usage, alongside broad experience including real estate operations.

In the past, his persistence led him to invent a pencil for mark sheets, which was featured on television and in exam magazines.

What interests him most now is not the tool itself, but finding the charm and potential inside people.

Alongside training work, he is collecting 968 words of praise to better identify people's strengths. He loves the moment a single word changes someone's expression.

He aims to provide training that avoids difficult terms and helps even people uneasy about AI feel that they can use it.

Based in Osaka, he works both in person and online.

He wants to gently support people who are working hard, want to change, or feel uneasy about AI.

I would rather be someone who can recover than someone perfect. Even if I cannot become extraordinary, I want to be someone who energizes others.
